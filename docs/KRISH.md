- **Building something new.** First choice for a free Tuesday (A1). Fire comes from "making something exist that did not exist yesterday" (R11.3 rank 1).

### What drains him

- **Teaching people who do not want to learn.** This is the real drain, not repetition itself (R1.1 rank 1). Repetition to a hungry room was his best day.
- **Selling, especially the chase and an unproven product.** Selling is the most dreaded part of a project (A6 rank 1); the first customer conversation ranks last among things he looks forward to (A5).
- **Maintaining what he built** (A3 rank 2, A6 rank 4).
- **Working alone.** Ranked dead last of four ways to work, below leading a large team (E1).
- **Media politics.** Twenty years fighting for open web publishing: "a world that would drain me mentally" (R4.2 note).
- **The work he fears ending up in:** "installing claude for businesses and managing people to use it all day long", with people who do not want to learn (A4 note, R2.5 rank 1).

## Acquisition doctrine

How Krish finds customers. This applies to Mindmake and to every product.

1. **No cold outbound.** Warm intros from his network, the room (keynotes, private sessions, the podcast), and published thinking only (Rule 2; E5 rank 1 "warm intros from your network"; cold outbound ranked last). Cold email is retired across the OS (Acquisition OS v1.1, Gate 1 ruling, Krish, 2026-07-06, recorded in `docs/MINDMAKE_OS_ARCHITECTURE.md` section 11.5).
2. **Channels he believes in:** search and AI answer visibility (SEO and GEO), organic social, small paid social, and his own Substack, cross-driving traffic between them. Stated 2026-08-04.
3. **Map the buyer's touchpoints first.** For each product, map where its buyer already goes, then cover those places as cheaply as possible. Control Center holds this map (Growth, the places map).
4. **Draft for approval. Nothing sends on its own.** Agents research, qualify and draft. Krish approves sends, in batches. "Drafts never send. Krish or the partner sends." (Sheet 4, section 4, "Approval walls")
5. **Paid spend is capped and gated.** Paid tests are capped at $500 a month across all products and start only after revenue is flowing through owned or earned channels (Acquisition OS v1.1, Gate 4). Agents never spend money. Spend is Krish's action.
6. **Publishing fills the room. It is not the swing.** "Publish to fill the room. Do not mistake publishing for the swing." (Sheet 1, section 3; R12.1 rank 4)

**That tension is resolved by Krish's ruling, not by rewriting the rules.** On 2026-10-06 he placed the portfolio inside the mission.
