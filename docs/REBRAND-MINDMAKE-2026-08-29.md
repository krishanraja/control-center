## The naming law, applied to the OS

- The business is **Mindmake**. Never "Mindmaker", never "The Mindmaker", never
  "Mindmaker Live", never "Mindmake AI".
- The advisory site is **mindmake.co**. `themindmaker.ai` and `www` variants are
  redirects and infrastructure. They are never named in copy.
- The operating system is **mind/make OS**. Stylised with the slash in prose and any
  public copy. Safe-slugged as `mindmake-os` in filenames, repo paths, skill slugs and
  database values.
- The product is **CTRL**, at `ctrl.mindmake.co`.
- The publication is **Mindmake's publication**. It runs exactly two channels,
  **The Money of AI** and **Built with AI**. There is no third channel and no other
  channel name.
- **Mindmaker LLC** is the registered legal entity. It appears only where the law wants
  the registrant named. It is not a brand.
- **`krish@themindmaker.ai` remains the only published contact address.** Decided
  29 August 2026: the mailbox migration is not being undertaken yet. This is a contact
  endpoint, not a name, so it does not breach the naming law. Do not publish
  `krish@mindmake.co` until the mailbox exists.
- `mindmakerlive.substack.com` is the publication's hosting address, not a name.
  Nothing in copy calls the publication by it.

**One exception, and only one: a verbatim quote is never edited.** A reference who said
"Mindmaker" still says it. The quote carries the old name. Nothing around it does.

## Acquisition doctrine

**No cold email.** Standing rule, reaffirmed 29 August 2026 and consistent with the
north-star decision rule that anything requiring sustained cold outbound will not
happen.

The machinery is repointed, not retired:

- **Warm intros and published thinking are the only two sales motions.**
- **Cold contact is legitimate for one purpose only: booking podcast guests.** That is
  an ask, not a sale.
- Every commercial approach routes through the existing network or inbound.
- SEO and GEO, social, and the Substack carry the top of funnel.

**Autonomy is raised.** Guest booking, warm-intro surfacing and meeting preparation run
without Krish in the loop. He sees one daily list: the people to actually talk to. The
authority boundary is unchanged, nothing is sent, published or scheduled without
per-asset approval.

**The three velocity metrics the OS optimises for**, in priority order:

1. **Pitch meetings for Mindmake** booked with the right people.
2. **Podcast guests** confirmed for Built with AI and Signal & Noise.
3. **Content published** against the two channels.

Everything else is instrumentation for those three.

## Who the OS targets

From canon. A founder, principal, portfolio owner, investor or senior commercial leader
who can move a decision on their own, and whose decisions are big enough that getting
them right matters.

Strongest situations:

- a healthy or fast-growing AI company whose product, price, positioning, people or
  company design has to keep up with its own market;
- an established internet, software, media, data, content, coaching or advisory business
  whose old commercial rules have stopped working;
- a well-connected principal whose scarce time is spread thin;
- a leader who wants an AI chief of staff, or a durable brain that makes their own
  judgement easier to use;
- a portfolio owner who wants more high-value time rather than a smaller inbox.

Write for ambition as much as for pressure.

**The north-star workbook is context about Krish as a person, not the go-to-market.**
Decided 29 August 2026. Its PE and VC commercial-diagnostic route, its pricing and its
growth-share structure are **not** the Mindmake offer and must not appear in ICP
scoring, agent briefs, outreach copy or lead routing. Its value is behavioural: how
Krish works, what drains him, what he will and will not sustain.

## Voice and register

- **British English.** Plain enough for a twelve-year-old. Short sentences.
- **We, not I.** The practice is the subject. Krish appears in exactly three places:
  the founder section, the framing of the proof, and inside verbatim quotes.
- **No em dashes anywhere.** Copy, docs, commits, chat, on-screen text, code.
- Never an eyebrow above a heading. Never doom, commands, boasting, or a cryptic
  headline.
- **The Kind Rule.** Irony points at claims, hype, incentives and decisions. Never at a
  named person's competence or character.
- **The Rigour Rule.** The register can be dry. The evidence handling is humourless.
  The joke can never be the finding.

**Banned language** in scripts, titles and captions: "AI transformation", "AI literacy",
"innovation", "capability building", "leverage AI", "in today's fast-moving landscape",
"time will tell", "the future of work", "game-changer", "unlock".
