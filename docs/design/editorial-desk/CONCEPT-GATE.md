# Editorial Desk concept gate

## Brief

Design a pull-only editorial decision state inside the existing Control Center Content flow. It must not become a new room, tab, queue or parallel suggestion surface. A raw signal remains neutral. The Money of AI and Built With AI assess it independently, so either, both or neither may produce an opportunity. Money requires a second-order commercial or labour mechanism. Built requires a workflow, implementation or artifact mechanism. The interface must show evidence, the strongest failure, safer and ambitious variants, and honest loading, empty, stale, partial and error states. Production choices remain locked until Krish approves completed content.

## Three interaction spines

### A. Proof Table

One source sheet remains fixed while Krish compares independent proof plates. Each plate exposes its mechanism, evidence, failure and message variants. This is the fastest comparison surface, but it risks feeling like another dashboard and makes a single next action harder when both lenses qualify.

### B. Cut Room

One signal moves through a deliberate editorial assembly sequence: establish the record, make independent lens calls, assemble the surviving route and approve completed content. A compact edit strip preserves prior calls. This makes the editorial thinking process visible and gives every state one clear next action.

### C. Story Railway

Evidence becomes a spatial Event to Receipts to Mechanism to Claim path. Earned Money and Built routes branch only after a complete mechanism. It makes causal reasoning unusually legible, but the metaphor adds learning and implementation cost, especially on mobile.

## Independent judgment

Two independent judges selected Cut Room. Their shared concern was that a literal Money-then-Built sequence could create anchoring and feel like a quota. Both recommended keeping the Cut Room spine while making the two lens passes optional and independent.

## Synthesized direction

Krish rejected a separate Editorial Desk destination because it duplicated the existing Content engine. The corrected rendered concept keeps Cut Room as the interaction model but makes it an expanded suggestion state inside the current Content room and composer path:

- one neutral source record;
- one Lens pass stage containing independent Money and Built calls;
- zero, one or two routes may survive;
- a compact Event to Evidence to Mechanism to Claim trace inside each route;
- strongest failure before route selection;
- safer, recommended and ambitious variants;
- one state-correct primary action;
- Assemble, completed-content approval and production unlock remain separate stages.
- no new Desk navigation item or parallel list;
- the existing Content suggestion opens into this state, then returns to the existing composer and approval lifecycle.

Borrowed elements do not replace the governing Cut Room interaction. The Proof Table contributes compact proof anatomy. The Story Railway contributes the causal trace and visible dead ends.
