# Control Center: where it is right now

## What it is

Control Center is where Krish Raja runs his businesses from: one dashboard over mind/make OS, the fleet of AI agents that does the operational work of growing his products and his publication, so that he only decides. A React and TypeScript app on Vercel, reading Supabase directly and writing back through thin serverless routes, plus the crons that keep the OS honest (health, money, events, goals, the weekly architecture record). The agents are not in this repo: they are Supabase rows, n8n workflows, Claude Code agents on a VPS and a few GitHub Actions. Nothing in it contacts Krish or sends anything on his behalf. The whole OS, its rules and its current truth: `docs/MINDMAKE_OS_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Who it is for and why it matters for Mindmake

Control Center is not sold. It is the proof behind Mindmake: the buyer Mindmake serves (the pilot_face lane in `docs/ICP.md`, and `product_icp` for each product: a senior leader who will not admit they are not ready for what is happening) is being asked to trust that one person can run real businesses on AI agents and stay in charge of them. This repo is that evidence, in public, with the failures left in. Krish ruled on 2026-10-05 that his ikigai, venture objectives, pricing and revenue are published here.

Angles a writer can use without asking Krish:

- **The OS never pings him, and agents never write into his Drive.** Pull-only since 6 September 2026; on 5 October every agent's Google Doc output was retired and Control Center became the one place agents report (architecture doc, section 0a.4 and 0a.5).
- **Honest numbers, even when they are small.** On 5 October the whole portfolio's lifetime revenue was reconciled across five Stripe accounts to $842.56 net from 9 payments, after an earlier "$1,244 across 20 charges" turned out to count failed charges and add Australian cents to US cents. The only live revenue is two founding members of the publication; CTRL has none. The dashboard says so rather than flattering it.
- **A green run that moved nothing is a failure.** The OS's most expensive bugs have been silent successes, and the fixes are in the open (architecture doc, section 13).
- **One move at a time.** Every tab now leads with one action and keeps the evidence one press away, the standard Krish set from the Growth tab.

Objection it answers: "AI agents in a real business just make noise." Here is the dashboard that made them quiet, and honest.
